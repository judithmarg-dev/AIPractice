# Mission

IceCreamStore runs a mobile-first staff-admin tool. Store staff and owners use it at the counter to manage the ice cream catalog, handle bookings, and monitor inventory and sales trends.

One user type: every screen is designed mobile-first for a staff member or owner, then scales up to the tablet at the counter and the manager's desktop.

Two goals matter equally:

- **Fast, frictionless operations.** Staff should manage catalog items, bookings, and inventory in a few taps on a phone or tablet, without hunting through menus.
- **Clear operational visibility.** Owners should see demand, order trends, and stocking signals without exporting data or building reports themselves.
